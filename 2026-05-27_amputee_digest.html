🔥 HIGH (7)

1 · Prosthetist-Led Comorbidity Screening RCT — A New Care Model  [A10]

Study — RCT (BMJ Open, Feb 27, 2026; NCT05410548). Adults with LL amputation randomized to prosthetist-administered comorbidity screening (PHQ-9, suicidal ideation, low back pain, peripheral vascular, protective-sensation screen) vs usual care during routine prosthetic follow-up.

Results — Significant improvement in comorbidity detection vs usual care. High acceptability by prosthetists and patients. Depression, neuropathy, and vascular disease identified and referred. Amputee comorbidity burden substantially exceeds general-population baseline.

Pearl — The prosthetist visit is often more frequent than any physician visit in this population. This RCT proves that window can be exploited for holistic screening.

Clinical takeaway — Add a brief structured screen (PHQ-9 + protective sensation + pedal pulse check) to every PM&R–prosthetics encounter checklist.

2 · TMR/RPNI — Prophylactic Pain Prevention in 45–100% of Patients  [A05]

Study — Comprehensive evidence review (PubMed 2025), synthesizing TMR and RPNI outcomes across multiple cohorts for postamputation neuroma pain, PLP, and prosthetic control.

Results — Neuroma pain improved in 75–100%; PLP improved in 45–80%; NRS reduction 2.4–6.2 points. Prophylactic use: neuroma-pain absent in 48–100%, PLP absent in 45–87% at follow-up.

Pearl — The optimal window for nerve management is the OR. Every PM&R consult on a pre-op or early post-op amputee is a chance to influence surgical planning.

Clinical takeaway — At first PM&R contact, document whether TMR/RPNI was performed; if not, initiate surgical consultation for salvage consideration.

3 · Transhumeral OI + TMR: Combined Surgical Approach Outperforms Socket Controls  [A02/A05]

Study — Preliminary observational cohort (PM&R, May 2025), n=3 transhumeral amputees: combined osseointegration + TMR with myoelectric prosthesis vs matched socket controls.

Results — Higher BAM-ULA scores and longer daily prosthetic use hours vs controls. OI eliminates socket pistoning/skin breakdown; TMR provides intuitive myoelectric signal quality.

Pearl — OI + TMR is a “double upgrade” for transhumeral amputees — this combination may represent state-of-the-art care for an underserved population.

Clinical takeaway — Transhumeral amputees should have early multidisciplinary OI + TMR feasibility planning before socket problems accumulate.

4 · PROCESS-Guided Primary TMR/RPNI: Prevention at the Source  [A05]

Study — PROCESS-framework case series (PubMed 2025), primary TMR/RPNI at time of amputation across multiple etiologies. Feasibility documented; early return to prosthetic rehab without significant pain burden.

Pearl — The most important time to prevent phantom pain is before it starts. Pre-amputation PM&R consult can change the surgical plan.

Clinical takeaway — For planned amputations (DM/PAD, oncologic), contact the surgical team before the OR to request concurrent TMR/RPNI consultation.

5 · Diabetes Doubles 5-Year Mortality After Major Amputation — Welsh Cohort (n=2,542)  [A06]

Study — Population-based cohort (PubMed 2025), n=2,542 major LL amputees in Wales.

Results — 5-year mortality: 67.0% with diabetes vs 57.1% without (HR 1.62, p<0.001). Paradox: reduced 30-day mortality with diabetes (hospital management effect). Long-term follow-up gap is the critical vulnerability.

Pearl — Early post-discharge looks deceptively good for diabetic amputees — the 5-year gap catches up. PM&R follow-up intensity at 6-month intervals is the intervention point.

Clinical takeaway — Schedule PM&R follow-up at 3, 6, and 12 months explicitly for diabetic amputees at inpatient discharge. Flag high-risk in EMR.

6 · What Counts as Recovery? LMIC Prosthetic Rehab Outcome Domains (SR)  [A11]

Study — Qualitative systematic review (BMJ Open, Jan 28, 2026; DOI 10.1136/bmjopen-2025-109817). ENTREQ-compliant, Fewins/Ostler/Donovan-Hall.

Results — Central domain: durable prosthesis enabling work and daily living. Secondary: social acceptance, independence. Western frameworks (K-level, 6MWT) miss highest-priority LMIC domains.

Pearl — K3 walking is not the goal in Kampala. Employment is. Foundational paper for LMIC prosthetic program design and WHO Rehab 2030 alignment.

Clinical takeaway — When consulting on global prosthetic programs, advocate for work participation and community role endpoints, not just mobility metrics.

🌍 Global lens

7 · GHOST EEG-BCI + VR for Phantom Limb Pain: Pilot Neurofeedback Trial  [A04]

Study — Single-center pilot (Frontiers in Human Neuroscience, Apr 7, 2026; DOI 10.3389/fnhum.2026.1697837). n=7 chronic upper-limb PLP patients. GHOST portable EEG-BCI + immersive VR; 10 sessions/2 weeks. Patients control a virtual limb via real-time motor imagery.

Results — Safety/feasibility confirmed. Short-term analgesic effects, particularly for paroxysmal pain. Supports sensorimotor re-engagement targeting maladaptive cortical reorganization.

Pearl — This device lets the patient control a virtual limb with their motor cortex — mechanistically distinct from passive mirror therapy. May be more durable for refractory PLP.

Clinical takeaway — For upper-limb amputees with chronic refractory PLP, BCI-VR is an emerging complementary non-pharmacologic option. Watch for trial enrollment opportunities.

8 · Osseointegration Systems: Are We on the Edge of a New Era? (Review, JOSR Jan 2026)  [A03]

Comparative review of OPRA, ILP, OPL, ITAP systems. OPRA cohort: 92% success at 5 years, sustained benefit at 15 years. Outcomes consistently superior to socket across all systems. Pearl: Technology is ready; the bottleneck is patient selection and equitable access to OI centers.

9 · Microprocessor Knee: Immediate PRO Gains Within 4 Weeks (n=29)  [A10]

Prospective multi-center (Apr 2025, n=29 TFA). ABC, RNLI, PEQ, 2MWT all significantly improved at 4 weeks (p<0.05). ABC ↔ 2MWT r=0.62. Physical activity (step count) unchanged short-term. Pearl: Confidence improves before behavior follows — use PROs at 4 weeks as the early signal.

10 · Osseointegrated Prostheses in TFA: Prospective Functional Outcomes (2025)  [A03]

Prospective study of functional PROs post-OI surgery in TFA. Multi-domain improvements; complication rates lower than prior series. Pearl: The “pioneer complication era” of OI may be passing as patient selection and technique mature.

11 · Growth and Prosthetic Provision in Pediatric Amputees: Cambodia (A09/A11)

Mixed-methods, Cambodia. Physical growth drives progressive socket failure and length discrepancy; LMIC supply chains cannot support frequent replacement. Pearl: Pediatric amputees in LMIC settings age out of prostheses faster than they can access replacements — a silent crisis.
